# About

I'm 65. Semi-retired, mostly by circumstance.

Last year I tried Amazon KDP — the way most people do. Watched the YouTube videos, opened Canva, started building. Spent 10 weeks on my first book. Got rejected 12 times before it was finally approved. Not because the book was bad. Because nobody told me about margins, bleed, embedded fonts, or trim size until Amazon sent me a vague email that explained nothing.

I did two more books after that. More rejections. Same reasons.

Eventually I knew enough to stop getting rejected — but I'd wasted months learning things that should have taken an afternoon.

That's when I started building manu2print.

The idea was simple: upload your PDF before Amazon sees it, find out exactly what's wrong, fix it before the rejection comes. No vague emails. No guessing. Just a clear list of what needs to change and where.

It took three months. Fifteen-hour days. Borrowed money. But it exists now — and it does what I needed it to do when I was starting out.

This newsletter is the other side of that. The things I learned along the way. What KDP actually checks. What Canva gets wrong. What the influencer videos skip. Practical, specific, no filler.

If you're trying to publish on Amazon KDP — welcome. You're in the right place.

— Manny
