The KDP rejection email says almost nothing useful.

"Your file does not meet our submission guidelines."

That's it. No page number. No specific issue. No fix instructions.

So you guess. You change something. You re-upload. You wait three days.

Rejected again. Same email.

None of them are hard to fix — once you know which one you have.
