# Tool Ideas Backlog

New tool ideas for manu2print from 3x-weekly DYOR. Zed reviews these; nothing gets built without his go-ahead.

## Ideas

- **Idea:** KDP Rejection Email Decoder
  **Pain it solves:** Authors paste KDP's vague rejection notice and get the likely cause in plain English, a fix checklist, and a pointer to the $9 Print Ready Check to confirm the file is clean before resubmitting.
  **Free or paid:** free lead magnet (email the full fix checklist; CTA into the $9 checker)
  **Effort:** small
  **Why now:** Under the new 2-titles-per-format-per-week create/submit caps (Sept 21 and Oct 5, 2026), a rejected upload costs a scarce weekly slot. Vague rejection emails and "disappointing customer experience" notices are a recurring KDP Community and Trustpilot complaint, and "KDP rejected" fix guides are a busy search niche.
  **Found:** 2026-10-10

- **Idea:** Cover PDF vs. Interior Match Check
  **Pain it solves:** The author uploads their finished wrap-cover PDF, enters page count, trim and paper type, and the tool checks the actual PDF dimensions and spine width against what KDP expects, plus the no-spine-text-under-79-pages rule and the barcode clear zone.
  **Free or paid:** add-on to checker (free dimension-only check as a teaser)
  **Effort:** medium
  **Why now:** Cover size not matching page count is the most cited reason for cover rejections in 2026 rejection guides. Competitors (bookow, KDPEasy, Self Publishing Titans) only generate templates or calculate sizes; none validate the finished cover file.
  **Found:** 2026-10-10
